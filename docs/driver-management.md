# Driver and hardware management

## Supported targets
- Intel integrated graphics
- NVIDIA legacy and modern GPU support
- Thunderbolt docking
- Touchscreen panels
- Ethernet and WiFi
- Webcam and audio devices
- USB peripherals

## Driver manager responsibilities
- Detect hardware profiles
- Choose stable open-source drivers
- Provide fallback packages for low-end devices
- Offer per-device repair and reboot recommendations
- Log driver signing and verification status
